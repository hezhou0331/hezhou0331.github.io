👋 About Me

I am an undergraduate in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, currently working in Ning Ding's research group.

I am especially interested in using agents to control robots: how they can understand tasks, plan actions, and interact with the physical world. I am also interested in reinforcement learning (RL), vision-language-action models (VLA), world models, and 3D vision, and how these approaches can help robots perceive their surroundings, anticipate changes, and learn to accomplish tasks through interaction.

I am still at the beginning of my research journey and enjoy exploring new questions through learning and hands-on work. Outside research, I enjoy table tennis and volunteering. Feel free to reach out for a game, a meal, or a chat about research and volunteer activities!
