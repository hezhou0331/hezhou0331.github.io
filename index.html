👋 About Me

I am an undergraduate in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University.

My research interests include visual representation learning, video generation, and embodied learning. I am interested in learning geometry and motion from visual observations and using these representations for robot action prediction and generalization. My recent work focuses on motion representations for world action models and stereo video VAEs.

Outside research, I enjoy table tennis and volunteering. Feel free to reach out for a game, a meal, or a conversation about research and volunteering!
